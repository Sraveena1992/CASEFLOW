About CASEFLOW

From messy situations to traceable decisions.

CASEFLOW is a role-based case workspace for teams that need to understand what happened, what evidence supports it, what is still blocked, and who should act next.

◎

One reliable case record

Keep ownership, status, blockers, evidence and updates together in a structured record.

⌁

Evidence before conclusions

Make supporting information and missing details visible so reviewers can assess a case with context.

✓

Human-reviewed decisions

Keep important changes reviewable and preserve a history of actions instead of silently changing records.

AI-assisted and agentic capabilities will be described as available only after their live integrations and workflows have been verified.

Why CASEFLOW

Because a task is not resolved just because someone took an action.

Important work gets lost between scattered information, unclear ownership, missing evidence and decisions that are hard to trace. CASEFLOW is being built to close that gap: one living record from the first description of a situation through review, follow-up and a verifiable outcome.

◎

Keep the full context

Bring the situation, owner, status, blocker, evidence and next action together instead of searching across disconnected conversations.

⌕

Know what is known

Keep evidence visible, identify missing information and avoid turning assumptions into facts.

↗

Make progress traceable

Give every case a clear next step and preserve a reviewable record of updates and decisions.

CASEFLOW's principle: an attempted action is not a verified outcome. Important decisions remain under human authority.

Core features

A case workspace built around clarity and accountability.

These are the product capabilities and design principles. Availability depends on the current deployed version and connected services.

▣

Living case records

Structured situation, case state, blocker, next action and ownership in one record.

◈

Evidence and history

Attach supporting information and retain a chronological record of case events.

✓

Role-based review

Separate Admin and Account Manager responsibilities, with human review for important changes.

⌁

AI-assisted proposals

Prepare structured transition proposals and check required fields before a person reviews them. The current validator does not independently authenticate evidence or approve a transition.

↻

Follow-up clarity

Keep blockers and next actions visible so a case can move forward deliberately.

🛡

Controlled agent testing — planned

A dedicated Agent Testing workspace is planned for bounded test runs, security and reliability checks, diagnostics and evidence reports. It is not yet represented as a live capability.
